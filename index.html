🛡️ Money Moves for Young Adults

The best time to look at life insurance is while you're young and healthy. Rates are based on your age and health when you apply, and health can change without warning. Life insurance may also help if a parent co-signed your student loans.

Why students ask about it:

  • Lock in insurability while you're healthy
  • Cover co-signed student loans so family isn't stuck with them
  • Some permanent policies build cash value you can borrow against later
  • Builds a base for your future family or business
